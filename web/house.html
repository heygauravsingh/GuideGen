One promise, and it's the whole point

Every tool here runs on your machine. Not "encrypted in transit", not "deleted after 30 days" — it doesn't leave in the first place.

Nothing is uploaded

Your files, screenshots and recordings stay on your computer. When something does need to leave — publishing a guide as a shareable link — you press a button that says so, and only that one thing goes.

No account for the sake of it

If a tool doesn't need to know who you are, it won't ask. GuideGen needs one only because it can publish and share.

Boring on purpose

No subscription to forget, no dashboard to check, no AI wrapper around a text box. A small thing that does one job and stays out of your way.
