FlowScribe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Summary: FlowScribe collects nothing, transmits nothing, and has no server. Everything it records stays in your own browser on your own computer.

What FlowScribe stores

When you record a guide, FlowScribe saves the following to your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your own device:

  • Screenshots of the pages you were on while recording
  • The text description generated for each step, which you can edit
  • The URL and page title of each recorded step
  • The position and size of the element you clicked, used to draw the highlight
  • Your guide titles and step ordering

What FlowScribe does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask you to sign in
  • It does not send your screenshots, text, URLs or any other data anywhere
  • It makes no network requests whatsoever — there is no server to contact
  • It contains no analytics, telemetry, tracking or crash reporting
  • It does not read page content other than the label and bounds of the element you click
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, because it never receives any

Text-to-speech narration

The spoken narration in video exports is generated entirely on your own machine by a speech engine bundled inside the extension. The text of your guide is never sent to a cloud text-to-speech service.

Where your data goes when you export

Exported guides — HTML, Markdown, PDF, PowerPoint and video files — are written directly to your computer's downloads folder by your browser. FlowScribe never uploads them. Once you share an exported file, that file is under your control and this policy no longer governs it.

Recording other people's information

Screenshots may capture whatever was on screen, which can include personal or confidential information belonging to you or to others. FlowScribe provides a redaction tool that pixelates selected regions in every export, and password fields are masked when captured. You remain responsible for what you record and for what you choose to share, including under any applicable data-protection obligations.

Permissions

FlowScribe requests access to all websites because it cannot know in advance which application you will want to document — it may be any internal tool or dashboard. The recorder is inert unless you have explicitly started a recording.

Deleting your data

Delete individual guides from the FlowScribe editor. To remove everything, uninstall the extension — Chrome discards its local storage with it. No copy exists anywhere else.

Children

FlowScribe is a workplace documentation tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If FlowScribe ever gains features that transmit data — for example optional hosted guide sharing — this policy will be updated before those features ship, and any such feature will be opt-in.
